Fatos sobre o VALID-71

VALID-71 é um vírus cuja origem, ao contrário do que foi contado, não possui origem mágica e, muito menos, epidemiológica. Curiosamente, o vírus não é um vírus. "Ele" não foi criado por mutações genéticas ao longo dos anos nem ao menos parece ter sido criado por magia. A cada avanço que faço em minha pesquisa, é como se uma porta fosse aberta mas a casa inteira fosse desabada.

O VALID-71 parece ignorar parcial ou até completamente tudo aquilo que é feito de matéria inorgânica. Não pense que se revestir de matéria inorgânica irá lhe salvar de ser consumido pelo mesmo. O VALID-71 consegue sentir a vida através de tudo. Se não fosse pelas medidas meticulosas da T.U.R. e pelo enorme gasto de recursos, o VALID-71 teria passado por esses portões em um piscar de olhos.

O nome "Vazio" vem tanto das manchas pretas que surgem pelo corpo quanto pelo vazio causado na alma do infectado. Começa a surgir lacunas na sua memória, causada por desligamento de neurônios, até que começa a afetar sua coordenação motora, com o “vírus” tomando o lugar dos antigos neurônios e controlando o que ele antes controlava. Sua manipulação de mana também é afetada diretamente, aos poucos, seu corpo não se torna mais seu, seus pensamentos não se tornam mais seus, até que você não é nada.

O “Vazio” quando termina de consumir uma pessoa, ele assume a consciência dela, sendo capaz de tomar ideias e funções básicas. Algumas pessoas fortes, quando consumidas pelo “Vazio”, podem ser vistas falando algumas palavras soltas, como se fosse o resquício da humanidade daquele corpo. Por conta disso, é possível ver corpos de infectados se mexendo, agindo com hostilidade.

Facts about VALID-71

VALID-71 is a virus whose origin, contrary to what was told, has no magical origin, much less an epidemiological one. Curiously, the virus isn't a virus. It wasn't created by genetic mutations over the years, nor does it even seem to have been created by magic. With every advance I make in my research, it's as if a door were opened but the whole house came tumbling down.

VALID-71 seems to partially, or even completely, ignore everything that is made of inorganic matter. Don't think that coating yourself in inorganic matter will save you from being consumed by it. VALID-71 is able to sense life through everything. If it weren't for T.U.R.'s meticulous measures and the enormous expenditure of resources, VALID-71 would have passed through those gates in the blink of an eye.

The name "Void" comes both from the black stains that appear across the body and from the void caused in the infected one's soul. Gaps begin to appear in your memory, caused by neurons shutting down, until it begins to affect your motor coordination, with the "virus" taking the place of the old neurons and controlling what it once controlled. Your mana manipulation is also directly affected; little by little, your body no longer becomes yours, your thoughts no longer become yours, until you are nothing.

When the "Void" finishes consuming a person, it assumes their consciousness, being able to take basic ideas and functions. Some strong people, when consumed by the "Void," can be seen speaking a few scattered words, as if it were the remnant of that body's humanity. Because of this, it's possible to see infected bodies moving, acting with hostility.

The Fateful Day

Roselynne and Violetta were a few kilometers from Shelter Cicada. It was night, so it was hard to see outside the vehicle. But, from afar, it was possible to hear sounds, like heavy footsteps treading this contaminated land. Steps coming closer and closer.

The moment her master noticed that sound, she pulled you close and quickly covered your mouth, curling into a sort of little spoon shape with you inside. She knew what was coming.

The sounds grew louder and louder. You could feel her heart racing with every step the creature took. In fact, at that moment, Violetta's heart was the only thing you could hear, because of how tightly she held you in her arms.

She knew a fact. A fact that you, at that moment, didn't know. Or at least hadn't stopped to think about. A vehicle of that size made a loud sound. Loud enough to echo across those devastated lands. This was a problem known to T.U.R., however, since the vehicle floated suspended in the air, they didn't see a problem regarding the attraction of creatures or of the "Void" itself toward it. The negligence of T.U.R.'s scientists toward this vehicle caused scars that will never leave you.

The creature, unknown, ceased its steps. Now, that silence was taken over by a more terrifying sound. Panting breath. A sniff. As if that creature were sniffing you out. By your luck, Roselynne, the creature was blind, making it harder for it to identify the vehicle. By her master's misfortune, she knew that two people wouldn't leave that vehicle alive. However much the creature ignored the vehicle, it would still follow it. They would need to leave the vehicle to reach the Refuge. Yet, that didn't stop her from trying.

She waited for the vehicle to get near the Refuge. Close enough to be visible with the eyes, even in the darkness. The creature seemed to just follow the vehicle, as if waiting for its prey to come off a tray and go onto its plate. Violetta then passed you instructions, whispering in your ear, a whisper so low that her voice seemed to hesitate to leave her mouth.

We need to get out of here as fast as we can. I'll open the vehicle's door and, at that moment, I'll put something heavy on the vehicle's accelerator. I'll make it louder and make the creature ignore us enough for us to reach the gates. - She tries to let out a smile, as if she were trying to ease some of your tension, but the smile isn't convincing. She gives Rose a quick kiss, similar to a goodbye kiss.

Violetta grabs some heavy utensils she had on the trip and prepares herself. She puts her hand on the door handle, looks at Rose, and nods, as if asking permission to do that. She then opens the door and instantly puts the utensils on the accelerator. She holds Rose's hand and jumps out of the vehicle, a few meters away from the Refuge's door. The creature, a creature of approximately 4 meters, goes running after the vehicle. Ignoring you momentarily. Violetta, still holding Rose's hand, sets off toward that door, running as fast as she could. Only 20 meters were left.

Violetta looks back and notices that the creature stopped chasing the vehicle. Now, it's heading toward you. Its heavy steps running toward the door draw the attention of the "Void." Violetta, holding Rose's hand as tight as a warrior holds his sword before a battle, looks desperately around, searching for something. A way out of that situation. A way to survive, not alone, but with Rose by her side. Only 8 meters were left.

The speed of the two wasn't greater than the creature's, but the distance between Violetta and Roselynne and the door was smaller than their distance from the creature. If they could pass through the gate fast enough, they would survive, together. Only 4 meters were left.

Violetta then lets go of Rose's hand and, defying everything that was expected in that situation, she freezes, a few meters from the door. She turns her face toward a dark place, where, even in the darkness, it was possible to see a silhouette.

- He.. He's here.

As soon as these words leave Violetta's mouth, she and Roselynne are hurled by the creature, thrown against the gate with a tremendous force. Rose, who was in front of Violetta, is the first to collide with that gate, hitting her head. Her vision blurs, her thoughts become confused, and she finds herself lying on that ground with the same infection that consumed that world, unable to move.

These are Roselynne's last memories before waking up inside a ward of Shelter Cicada, where she now had stains on her body, but no sign of her master.
