Capítulo 2 - Ouro

Apaga uma de suas memórias sobre o Citrine.

Agora que Violetta possuía a certeza que "Ele" era capaz de acessar qualquer memória, mesmo que não seja de imediato, ela criou dois modos de segurança. Todavia, caso "Ele" fosse capaz de ler essas memórias com o contexto necessário, então Violetta apagaria memórias chaves de sua pesquisa, para evitar que "Ele" as pegasse. Seriam informações que ela em um curto período de tempo conseguiria assimilar seus resultados. Isso seria um atraso em suas pesquisas, mas todo cuidado é pouco nesse momento.

Ao longo do tempo em que Violetta apagava suas memórias, ela ia perdendo algumas informações soltas. Para pessoas comuns, as memórias que Violetta perdia eram tão banais que seriam facilmente esquecidas em poucos dias. A assimilação com o Citrine a mantém viva para que possa continuar suas pesquisas, mas também a faz perder lentamente sua sanidade se questionando qual será sua próxima memória perdida. Violetta sabe que é um risco necessário a se correr. Afinal, os fins justificam os meios. É o risco necessário para salvar as pessoas, como ela sempre quis. Salvar sua amada. Entretanto, nesse momento, quem a salva? Quem sacrifica tanto sobre si mesmo por ela? Essas eram perguntas que Violetta não se faria, pois assumir o cargo que ela possui atualmente foi uma escolha consciente dela.

Ao longe, era possível sentir uma presença se encaminhando até o local onde estava. Um sentimento familiar, porém que Violetta não sabia definir. Ao lado dessa presença, um ser totalmente assimilado à Profanidade. Ela pensava ser mais um sacrifício que havia chegado recentemente àquele local, mas seu coração batia mais forte sem ela saber a razão. Ela sentia a presença se aproximando aos poucos dela, e a cada passo da pessoa, o coração de Violetta batia mais rápido. Aquela criatura agora estava abaixo dela, e Violetta a sentia de longe, até escutar uma voz. A voz de sua amada.

Naquele momento, os pensamentos de Violetta eram tão rápidos quanto as batidas de seu coração. Ela cogitava ser um truque Dele, cogitava ser realmente Roselynne, e se perguntava de várias formas diferentes como ela havia chegado até aquele local, se ela estava usando a proteção apropriada, se ela foi enganada, se "Ele" estava por perto e ela foi coagida por ele.

There are few things I like in this world, but I can assure you that people who meddle in my affairs are not one of them.

As He walks slowly toward the two of them, vines sprout from the ground, grabbing Violetta by the neck and slowly suffocating her.

Perhaps by some cosmic irony, at this moment I happen to be short on people for a particular event. I am in search of a young one or a mentor. And what luck of mine to find both on my territory.

Violetta struggles and fights to free herself from His vines, but her efforts bear no success. The vines, also bathed in that flow, now tinge the white of her skin with a bluish tone.

What d… do you want..?

Ah, I am not the kind of person who puts his cards on the table so easily. You are someone special, I know that. And I know your life out here is worth far more than hers. Therefore, I only want you to choose. Who will come with me? You? Or her?

Violetta stays unconscious for a while. She begins to wake up, chained to the walls. On her skin, stains covering a large part of her pale face. Around the place, walls made of stone can be seen. A somewhat bluish stone. A cyan color. The only perceptible color in a vastness of black and gray. The colors become jumbled. Her discernment of things is clouded by that wave of abstract thoughts.

You're awake. - A male voice comes out of the darkness of the room. - I thought it would take a few more days.

The silhouette of that man stands up. A darkness darker than the absence of light in that room. The only thing that can be seen in the darkness are His eyes. Shining and watching her the way a predator stares at its prey.

Eat. - He hands her a plate with a steak whose doneness is nowhere near acceptable. The cutlery is dirty and rusty. She is not given a glass of water. Only meat bordering on raw. - You need to eat.

Violetta's mouth opens with some difficulty.

So, you are Him, I presume. I did find it odd that there were thoughts distinct from my own ever since I stepped outside. What is the reason for that? Perhaps posters scattered around would already help with your publicity campaign.

The advantage of the "Void" - He gestures the quotation marks with his hands - is that there is no need for an introduction. For someone like you who hates wasting time, I imagine it is advantageous too.

You seem to know quite a lot about me, don't you? Maybe my fame isn't only on this side of the walls. - Her body adjusts itself on the hard cyan brick floor.

The reason you're still alive is exactly your fame, to tell you the truth. - He pulls a chair close to Violetta. - When I realized my companion was searching for the source of a noise, I wanted to know what it was. And I find you right outside the walls. What cosmic luck of mine.

And so? Did you bring me here for an autograph? Or are you parasocial and have erotic dreams about me? Because what I want to give you is neither of those things. Besides, for your information, I am already very happily married.

Oh no, Violetta, nothing attracts me to you besides your brilliant mind. So... how about I make some use of it?

Use..? What an inelegant conversation. Use it how exactly? Because all my research is on how to stop the "Void", and not on how to give it a happy ending.

Well, here's a curiosity, VALID-71 researcher. This "Virus" has different properties when combined with certain materials. Pure, it consumes. However, as you must already know, when bound to gold it grows slowly, besides increasing the life of living beings. But there's the special one, isn't there? The one you used on your assistant? The one that kept her stable against the Profanity..

He approaches her.

- You discovered something interesting, and I want to invest in that knowledge. You discovered how to stop the infection, and I want to know how.

Chapter 1 - Longing

It was only her second day awake inside what seemed like a dungeon, yet she had already lost track of how much time might have passed. Now, she was no longer chained, but rather free in a cube of cyan bricks too despicable for her to call a bedroom. In that place there was a table with alchemical instruments, an old chalkboard, and a makeshift bed on the floor, along with a broken mirror.

She picked up one of the mirror shards to examine herself. Nothing seemed abnormal about her except for the stains on her face. At that moment, her mind loses control for a few seconds. The doubts about Roselynne. The opinions about her appearance. The fear of her future. Nothing Violetta could not control. It was not the time for thoughts like that, after all. He brought her here for a reason. Even if fulfilling that reason does not guarantee her freedom, it was what she needed to focus on now.

She glances at her jewel. The jewel that ties her to Roselynne. The jewel's shine makes her feel calm, even if a little. Violetta, more than anyone, knew it could all be a trick. A hallucination caused by the VALID-71 present in her body. But there was no reason to question the crystal's veracity. Even if the jewel were not truly lit, Violetta would remain in the same place, with the same purpose. It was rational to keep believing in Roselynne's life. It was necessary to be able to fulfill her role there. It was what she needed. It almost seemed like a bad-taste joke how those lifeless walls reminded her of the person who made her world shine. The cyan color now brought Violetta doubts, and not comfort.

She makes her way to the table, going toward a folded paper near the utensils.

"Hello, Violetta. I hope you have rested enough. In this room, there is everything you will need to put together the recipe you made years ago. Sorry for the delay in reviving you, but otherwise you would serve purposes that do not value your skills.

I have stagnated the VALID-71 inside your body. It will do nothing to your mind or to you unless I want it to, so let's try to be cooperative, understood? Once a week, I will visit your place to ask about the progress of your research. I will provide what is necessary, as long as you hold up your end.

I am looking forward to working with you, Dr. Carmine. Together, let's generate maieutics."

Years? What do you mean, "years"?

She searches her mind for a logic to understand, but an answer is delivered to her. She had spent two years unconscious, trapped in that cube in a vegetative state while the world outside kept moving. While her beloved remained alive. But it was not the time to think about that. There was a reason she had been unconscious for so long, as if He were waiting for the best moment to revive her. A date surfaces in her mind, and at that moment everything becomes clear.

Today is the day of the 13th Expedition, and Violetta knows it was a convenient time to wake up. He plans to do something with that batch of people who will leave the refuges. Perhaps use them for experiments, perhaps fulfill the macabre goal He once threatened to carry out with Roselynne. Whatever it is, she will have to be faster. If what He said is true, touching the "Void" no longer brings any harm to Violetta. And that is the best advantage she could have right now. If she studies the "Void" enough, maybe she will be able to create her own variation. The variation that fights VALID-71 itself. All vaccines use the characteristics present in the virus. In the case of the VALID, which does not present exactly as a virus, it would be a long research to figure out how to do it. But it is the best path.

Our last hope for humanity, Violetta Carmine, fervently begins to study with the objects she is given. She cannot cling to other people right now. She cannot cling to her doubts, her feelings, her fears. Every second matters.

Every second matters so that Roselynne can stay alive.

Chapter 2 - Gold

Violetta had already been in that laboratory for some time. The lack of light in that place harmed Violetta's mind, which no longer knew whether days or months had passed since she had been there. Her research advances quickly every day. Now that Violetta no longer needs to touch the "Void" with so much protection and care, she was able to carry out experiments that were previously impossible, not only because of the way they had to be done but because a single minimal error with a small sample would already be enough to bring down that entire refuge.

Violetta, through her studies, managed to understand that the VALID is not just a moss or something separate. It is something alive in itself, generated by something. Born from something. As if the "Void" were no different from a great spider's nest. It is a morbid, disgusting union of something living and sentient, created to be that way in a womb. When Violetta discovered this, she felt disgust. Disgust at what was on her skin, as if her skin were now a new nest for these parasites to consume. But, as disgusting as that idea was, it was still a good discovery. If that was something alive, it could be replicated. If it can be replicated, it can be altered in its generation. And if it can be altered in its generation, it can be created biologically to fight against the VALID.

Violetta, then, began two separate researches. One destined for Him, since that was the reason He brought her, and one destined for herself. She would create a way to potentiate the "Void", at the same time that she created something that could go against it. By instinct and also by a detail, she named that biological anti-VALID compound "Citrine", in honor of her beloved.

Her Citrine research advanced well against what Violetta was studying, until the day that would change all her research arrived. He arrives at Dr. Carmine's laboratory holding a huge brown sack.

Hello, Doctor. Got a moment?

I'll have to check my super busy schedule. Today was the day I was going to the market to buy pitanga. Why don't you come back next week? - She gives a forced smile.

Good to know you haven't lost your sense of humor, Doctor. But maybe you'll like the present I brought you.

He opens the sack, revealing an overwhelming amount of gold inside.

I got this from an acquaintance. Approximately 2 tons of gold. I think this should help with your research.

Huh. What acquaintance carries 2 tons of gold around?

A special acquaintance. But your job is not to ask questions about where I got this material. I want you to analyze the gold and join it to the VALID. The Profanity by itself cannot absorb the gold that well, and it is hard to get large samples without being repelled. But this should be enough.

Understood. - Violetta's face closes up when she hears His request.

Try to be as quick as possible. If you manage to assimilate it perfectly with the gold, it will help me in other matters too.

May I ask what matters those are?

You may. Do you want to?

… No. I'll work on what I need to.

Thank you for your unconditional support, Dr. Carmine. - He smiles.

He leaves the laboratory, locking the door once more. Violetta finds herself in a situation that seems to have no way out. Not cooperating means losing the only chance to save this world from the VALID. In a short time inside that laboratory, Violetta made greater progress than years inside the refuges, and it would not be long until she could create what she wished. At the same time, agreeing to this plan means accepting to kill refuges. The gold is the only resource around them that prevents it from advancing at this moment. If the gold is not a protective measure, the refuges will fall quickly.

Violetta found herself at an impasse. She had no right choice, only the choice with the fewest collateral damages. Looking at lives, one life in exchange for thousands is the best choice. On the other hand, her life was what could protect thousands. Violetta would have to put herself above many people in exchange for saving others. Deep down, she knew the choice she wanted to make, but she did not want to accept the consequences it would bring.

She picks up the first gold bar in her hands and holds it with a grief akin to how a killer holds a knife, and takes it to the workbench to study it better. She will try to go as fast as she can with her Citrine studies, but deep down she knows that the lives she may save in the future will not make up for the deaths she will cause now. But she cannot act based on her feelings. Especially not in a decision like this one.

Deep down, she only thought of people like Roselynne who would die before meeting their Violettas. But that was not something Violetta allowed herself to think about.

Chapter 3 - Violetta's Assimilation

"Assimilation. The act or effect of assimilating, meaning the incorporation, absorption, or comprehension of information, nutrients, or cultural elements by a larger system. It refers to the cultural adaptation of minorities, the integration of new knowledge, or the conversion of food into body substances."

31 days. The exact time, calculated mentally by Violetta, of how long she has been in this place. Her research involving the gold is almost complete. It did not take long for her to manage the assimilation of the VALID with the gold. Sometimes, while working with that assimilation, she kept wondering whether Roselynne would be severely affected by it. Unfortunately, for Dr. Carmine, all that was left to her was the hope that Roselynne would stay as safe as possible.

With the progress of the Citrine studies, Violetta managed to extract some information concerning the VALID. One of them was the certainty that her beloved was alive, even though she could not pinpoint her location. The reverse engineering Violetta did on the VALID allowed her to feel whoever was connected to it. Something that, currently, only He could do. Violetta, in a few days, managed to become the person with the greatest progress on the Profanity and the second greatest in knowing the truth behind it, trailing behind a historian whose presence Violetta was never able to understand.

The realization that her beloved was alive had lifted a weight off her shoulders. The uncertainty, even though Violetta was an expert at setting her feelings aside during her research, consumed her little by little. It was almost morbidly funny how Violetta had never been destabilized by someone before. How longing was a feeling she had been completely unaware of until she met Roselynne.

The certainty about her beloved's well-being acts as a small fuel. She needed, now more than ever, to advance the Citrine studies. She needed to get out of there urgently to prevent the Profanity from consuming the refuges. It was only a matter of time before it happened. The gold no longer worked effectively, and not all refuges had the capacity to heat their exterior to the temperature necessary for the VALID to be extinguished. All she needed was a few more days. Just a few more weeks and-

Citrine, isn't it? Interesting research you've been doing, Dr. Carmine.

Without His presence being noticed, He stood leaning against a wall behind Violetta. He looked at her the way a scientist looks at their lab rat after the results come out as expected.

But I don't remember having given you that task. Perhaps there was some confusion. Perhaps I just was not clear before. I apologize if my words may have sounded confusing.

He plants his feet on the ground and begins to walk slowly toward Violetta with a disconcerting smile. Violetta, at that moment, thought of 1001 ways to refute Him. To disturb Him. But she knew that any wrong word spoken at that moment could cause not only her own death, but Roselynne's and, consequently, that of the rest of the refuges.

Ah, so many thoughts against me. None of them is good enough. None of them even explains your actions. But you don't have to just think, Violetta Carmine. Say what you think. I'm a good listener.

This was a research on reverse engineering of the VALID. I was studying it with the intent of actively going against it, like a reversible natural agent.

Violetta knew that lying in that situation would be useless. He seemed to know what she was thinking, and no one knows how long He has been observing her, if He ever stopped observing her at all.

Your honesty has its value. But you know... I don't like it when people mess with what is mine. You understand, don't you? The good side is that, even though I wish to tear your throat open right now, I need to be a rational person. You understand that very well.

And you think threats like that will give me any fear? You have control here, I understood that. You can do whatever you want to me. But that doesn't scare me. It is more like an obstacle. Like a stop sign on the road.

You have a sense of humor. I appreciate that. But let me just remind you of one thing you have already studied, Dr. Carmine.

With one hand, He firmly grabs Violetta's wrist. With the other, He holds her thin, pale fingers, wrapping his hand around them. Without blinking, He forces them back, leaving them completely turned against the back of Violetta's hand. She, at that moment, lets out a loud scream of pain but tries to hold it in as much as possible. If it is a reaction He wants, she will try her best not to give it to Him. She cannot let herself be dominated like that.

The Profanity can heal any wound. From an exposed fracture to the loss of a limb. With the right channeling, you can even achieve immortality with your body at its peak. In other words, Dr. Carmine, I can break you into as many pieces as I want and put you back together. And if I can do that to you, don't think I won't also do it to Roselynne.

Violetta can feel her fingers quickly returning to place, while her hand is taken over by a dark, cyan stain.

But thank you for your research, Doctor. That Citrine seems promising. If the Profanity manages to assimilate with it, I think there wouldn't be much of a way to find a "cure", would there?

Violetta stays silent while holding her hand. She would like to speak now. To say everything she thinks about Him. But she cannot take the risk. Not when so much is at stake.

I was thinking of doing this after a certain time, but you seem impatient. Maybe I can make a change in my schedule. Why don't we start your assimilation right away?

Assi..milation?

I, for example, am an assimilation of a black moss that existed in the caves even before the world was covered by the Profanity. I planned to assimilate you with the gold, but with something as great as the Citrine, there is no denying it. What cosmic luck of mine.

Violetta did not know yet, but her process of assimilation with the Citrine would be the most painful and agonizing moments of her life.

Chapter 4 - Reunion

It had been a while since Violetta had thought about the sun. Even though she was not its biggest fan, the light she could see from her window was missed. A walk with her beloved through the streets in their time off, enjoying a good restaurant and looking at Roselynne in her outfit, whose way of dressing she had thought over even too much. Violetta had never thought she would miss moments like that in her life, both because of the constancy she had with Roselynne and because she had never imagined being able to love someone the way she loves her.

Now, confined in a dark room, she wonders if she will ever be able to see the sun again. At least with her mind intact. She sighs. Thoughts like that will harm her discernment of things. The last thing Violetta can do at this moment is doubt her actions. She moves her hand to her face, with the palm of her hand covering the Citrine stains on her face. With a certain pain in her heart, she does it again. She erases one of her memories about the Citrine.

Now that Violetta was certain that He was capable of accessing any memory, even if not immediately, she created two safety modes. If He were capable of taking her memories and interpreting them the way she herself interprets them, she would think in a coded manner to make it harder to understand certain information. However, if He were capable of reading those memories with the necessary context, then Violetta would erase key memories of her research, to prevent Him from getting them. They would be pieces of information whose results she could assimilate in a short period of time. That would be a delay in her research, but no caution is too much at this moment.

Over the time Violetta erased her memories, she kept losing some loose bits of information. For ordinary people, the memories Violetta lost were so banal that they would be easily forgotten in a few days. But for her, who had never forgotten anything, forgetting certain things worried her. Especially memories she considered important, like her beloved's face.

The assimilation with the Citrine keeps her alive so she can continue her research, but it also slowly makes her lose her sanity, wondering what her next lost memory will be. Violetta knows it is a necessary risk to take. After all, the ends justify the means. It is the necessary risk to save people, as she always wanted. To save her beloved. However, at this moment, who saves her? Who sacrifices so much of themselves for her? Those were questions Violetta would not ask herself, for assuming the role she currently holds was a conscious choice of hers.

In the distance, a presence could be felt making its way toward the place where she was. A familiar feeling, yet one Violetta could not define. Beside that presence, a being fully assimilated to the Profanity. She thought it was another sacrifice that had recently arrived at that place, but her heart beat faster without her knowing the reason. She felt the presence drawing slowly closer to her, and with every step of that person, Violetta's heart beat faster. That creature was now below her, and Violetta felt it from afar, until she heard a voice. The voice of her beloved.

At that moment, Violetta's thoughts were as fast as the beats of her heart. She considered it being one of His tricks, considered it truly being Roselynne, and asked herself in several different ways how she had reached that place, whether she was wearing the proper protection, whether she had been deceived, whether He was nearby and she was coerced by Him.

However, with every word of her beloved, her mind calmed down. How could she have forgotten that beautiful face? The eyes she stared into every morning. No hallucination could replicate her beloved so well. At that moment, Violetta no longer missed the sun, for in front of her stood her greatest light.

When the conversation came to an end, Violetta was confident that the situation would change. Even if her beloved studied the Citrine and was coerced by Him, she would be safe. She herself will take care of the consequences her actions may have in the future. She will not give up so easily. Not now. Not when her fiancée crossed heaven and hell for her. Even if it hurts, even if it breaks every bone in her body, she will fulfill her promise to her beloved and wear an outfit that Roselynne would certainly question the aesthetic sense of her wife over, while her eyes are full of tears.
